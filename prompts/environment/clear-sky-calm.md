# Clear Sky & Calm

Fair-weather stillness across varied settings: gentle breeze, birdsong, and insects under open sky. This is the calm anchor against every storm file — even Level III never turns stormy, it is simply the most alive a calm day gets. Add `speech, voices` to Negative for empty scenes; drop it where a village, harbor, or courtyard backdrop is part of the entry.
