# Thunderstorm

Rolling thunder, lightning-adjacent rumble, heavy rain bands, and gusting wind combined into one loopable storm bed. Level III is the storm at peak violence but stays a steady loop, never a single strike one-shot. Add `speech, voices` to Negative for empty scenes; drop it where a village or camp backdrop is part of the entry.
