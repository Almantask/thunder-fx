# Status effects

Short ticks and start/stop cues for burn, freeze, stun, poison. Not music stingers — keep `TrackType: SFX`.

### Burn start
- Duration: 1.5s
- Negative: music, speech, singing

TrackType: SFX, clothes catching a small flame, close mic, dry studio, short decay

### Burn tick
- Duration: 1s
- Negative: music, speech, singing

TrackType: SFX, small fire tick on fabric, close mic, dry studio, fast decay
