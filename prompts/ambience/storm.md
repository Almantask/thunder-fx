# Storm

Weather as antagonist — thunder textures, rain, dark strings, timpani swells. Arc: I distant thunder → II driving rain → III the full tempest.

Instrumental D&D ambience for Stable Audio 3 Medium. Each cue starts with `TrackType: Music`. Cue lengths vary by intensity and take, up to the Stable Audio 3 Medium maximum of 380s (6m 20s). Adapted from the Sunder Suno library.
